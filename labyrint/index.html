Replanning

An agent walks the map with D* Lite while you add walls in front of it. It repairs its plan instead of starting over.

Controls

Drag on the map to draw walls. Shift or right-drag erases. Drag the white and red dots to move start and goal. With a result on screen, edits re-run the search at once.
